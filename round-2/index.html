OddsBoss: Round 2 Meta ads

Round 1 got cheap clicks but was optimised for landing page views, with no pixel. Round 2 runs the winning memes again, plus 11 new versions of them, optimised for people who start checkout. 13 ads in 2 ad sets, one campaign budget of $90 a day, US, age 21+.

Download all Round 2 ads (.zip)

Before you start

  • Wait for the owner's go-ahead that the pixel test passed. Don't publish before that.
  • Pixel: use PV1 Ads Meta Pixel (ID 4567744503371725). Not "PV Ads Meta Pixel".
  • In Events Manager, check that InitiateCheckout shows as received in the last 7 days. If it doesn't, open getoddsboss.com/start-now/ once on your phone and check again after 30 minutes.
  • Upload the ads exactly as they are. No added text, logos, stickers or music.

1. Campaign

  1. New campaign. Objective: Sales. Name: OddsBoss_US_Memes_Test2.
  2. Advantage campaign budget: ON, $90 per day. Bid strategy: Highest volume (no cost cap, no bid cap).
  3. A/B test: OFF. Special ad categories: none.

2. Ad sets (make 2, settings are identical)

  1. Names: OB_US_R2_Losses and OB_US_R2_Wins.
  2. Conversion location: Website. Pixel: PV1 Ads Meta Pixel. Conversion event: Initiate checkout.
  3. Attribution: 7-day click or 1-day view.
  4. Audience: Advantage+ audience ON. Under audience controls set Location: United States and Minimum age: 21. Leave suggestions empty.
  5. Placements: Advantage+ placements.

3. Ads

  1. Format: Single image. Upload the feed 4:5 file, then use "Edit placements" to put the stories 9:16 file on Stories and Reels.
  2. Paste the ad name, primary text, headline, website URL and URL parameters for each ad below. Paste the URL parameters exactly; don't add {{ }} macros or IDs.
  3. Advantage+ creative / creative enhancements: turn every option OFF. Music: OFF. Multi-advertiser ads: OFF.
